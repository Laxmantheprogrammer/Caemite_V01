# Caemite_V01
The newer model and framework for Caeminate , the next level , Caemite.

# Story 
So I was going to make the next version of Caeminate , which was to be called Caeminate V1.

Then Me and my friend decided , that instead of making many versions , we should just make the singular , best version , which would be called Caemite.

The short synopsis is that it takes in the data of different materials and their mechanical , radiational , thermal properties and reactions under vibrations and their atomic characteristics.

Then it trains on that data , and predicts a material which would perform good on those fields , then first cheap tests the proposed material , and if the material passes the cheap test , it uses the following _ simulators :

GEANT4
Quantum ESPRESSO
LAMMPS
CalculiX 

for the simulation of the material under different kinds of radiations , their atomic potentials and energies , their bulk properties , thermal properties , mechanical properties and reactions under vibrations respectively (CalculiX is planned to be used for the last 3)

Then we would get the data and predictions about the material.

This is very special to me , because the model does not predict a multilayered material , rather it is a single molecule , which would theoretically have good performance for those properties.

This might mean that the simulations might always make something unrealistic , or that the required margins might not all be reached , but that was never truly the goal of mine.

This is a fun project , and it is something I find meaningful , so I do it , and that is the majority of the reason.

As stated before in Caeminate V01 , I chose that project for two reason:

First it was an open problem
Second , I thought that , I can do this. 

And this is just the continuation of that project.

# Aim

The aim is to make a project , which can do the following things:

0. It should be able to work. And work in a reasonable manner.

1. It should be able to install with one singular command on the installer's PC , or atleast it should minimize the number of commands and steps the user has to go through.

2. It should first ask the user all the specifications they want , and then check whether they have all the tools in their PC appropriate for those specifications , and then install those tools first.

3. There should be a reasonable GUI , it would most likely be in the terminal itself , which would ask the user what they want , what they want to simulation , which properties they need , the ranges of tolerances they need , so on and so forth .

4. There should be good error and failure management.

Those are the ones I can think of rn. I will add the goals as we progress.
